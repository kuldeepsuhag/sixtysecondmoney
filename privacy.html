Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Sixty Second Money ("we") runs a YouTube channel and a private tool, the "Sixty Second Money uploader", that we use only to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

What the uploader accesses

With our permission, the uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails on the Sixty Second Money channel, and to read public information about trending videos. It is not offered to the public and is not used to access anyone else's account.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information from viewers or other users. The only Google account the uploader connects to is our own. Access tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are never shared.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own terms and Google's privacy policy also apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
